Mixed real handmade/MIDV training: completed pilot

Both blocks were trained/tuned independently using real camera pairs. No artificial degradation was added.
Data: 58 training pairs (5 handmade + 53 MIDV); 18 validation (2 + 16); 14 paired test (2 + 12).
Training sides/cards do not cross into validation or test. Handmade sides remain separate at user request; repeated printed content may cross splits.
Wiener: radius 3, K 0.3, no extra denoising/sharpening.
Restormer: step_2000; results/real_mixed_pilot/restormer/step_002000.pt.
Restormer training: 2,000 steps, learning rate 1e-5, 128px patches; source- and identity-balanced sampling; 75% text-centered patches.
Checkpoints and Wiener settings selected only on validation using the predeclared CER/SSIM rule.

INTERPRETATION AND LIMITS
These are exploratory held-out results on a reused benchmark, not an untouched confirmatory test.
Only two handmade sides qualify for paired test metrics. The broader handmade OCR test covers three sides, 20 photos including sharp/glare controls.
The paired MIDV test covers 12 photos of four cards; the broader MIDV OCR test covers 60 photos of five cards. Photos of a card are not independent identities.
SSIM is the mean over fixed valid native text-region image crops. Requiring reliable alignment preferentially excludes difficult captures; use the broader OCR test to assess more difficult photos.
Handmade transcriptions are assumed correct at user direction pending later review.
References are separate real camera captures, with residual blur, lighting differences and geometric interpolation. SSIM is similarity to those registered references, not certified blur-free truth.
Operational alignment gate: median residual <=1.5px and p90 <=3px among reliable fields, >=70% measurable, followed by visual review. Offset sensitivity does not certify all remaining alignment errors.
Native captured input pixels are retained; only reference images are warped. No per-method registration or brightness normalization.
CER is character-weighted over supplied fields, using Tesseract eng/PSM7. It is not whole-document recognition or automatic field detection.
MIDV overlaid name_code and secondary/holographic birth_date_2 fields were excluded uniformly before fitting. Pair coverage/field scope differ from earlier reports, so compare methods within this run.
Wiener and Restormer operate separately on the same original; they are not chained.
Original defaults and earlier checkpoints are preserved.
