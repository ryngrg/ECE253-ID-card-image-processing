Degradation-stratified OCR analysis

Filters and OCR outputs are unchanged. CER = pooled edits / reference characters.
Blur severity: existing training-only sharpness tertiles; not physical blur power.
Noise power: existing native-image Haar-MAD sigma squared, in 8-bit intensity squared.
A near-zero estimate does not establish a noise-free image. JPEG/quantization and blur affect the estimator.
Three noise bins are unavailable when training quantiles collapse.
Directional proxy: equal-field mean anisotropy vectors from gradient second moments.
Text previews smaller than 5 pixels in either dimension are excluded from direction scoring only.
High structure = above training 2/3 quantile; directions describe least-change axes in rectified text.
Letter shapes and layout also produce directional structure. These are NOT verified motion categories.
Reviewed blur types remain uncertain unless supplied separately through --labels.
No filter is selected using these groups. Test is a reused benchmark with only five cards.
Small subgroups can be dominated by card content. No statistical superiority is claimed.

Training directional threshold: 0.291886
Training noise sigma thresholds: None
